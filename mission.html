Teach Canary your missions

Canary recognises a mission from the enemies in the log. That fails for missions you can finish without a single shot, and for missions with completely generic enemies. The NPC chatter in local is the missing piece: it is unique per mission, and it even says when a mission is done. That line does not exist in the game log.

Pilot names stay here. You paste your whole local, and everything except the NPC lines is stripped in your browser before anything is sent. The preview shows you exactly what goes out.
Send as GitHub ticket

The quickest way

  1. Run the mission and keep the local window open.
  2. Afterwards click into local, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C.
  3. Paste here, add the mission name, send. Done.

The most valuable ones are missions Canary does not recognise, and those you can finish without combat. Exactly those fall through today.
